Lead to Returning Student Journey

This page explains, in simple English, what happens from the first lead stage until the student finishes a level and becomes either Renewal Pending or Paid in Full / Waiting for Round.

1. Lead Journey

The person enters the system as a lead. The team books the placement test, records the result, prepares the offer, and records payment.

  • New Lead
  • Test Booked
  • Tested
  • Offer Sent
  • Payment Recorded
  • Waiting for Round
  • Ready to Start
  • In Classes

2. Student Journey

After the student joins a class, they attend the level, receive grades and attendance tracking, and then the round is closed.

  • In Classes
  • Attend Sessions
  • Grade + Attendance
  • Round Closed
  • Promoted or Repeated

3. What Happens After the Level Ends?

Case A: Paid in Full

If the student already has prepaid credit for the next level, the system moves them to Waiting for Round. They do not need a new payment first.

Case B: Renewal Pending

If the student finished the level but has no remaining prepaid credit, the system moves them to Renewal Pending. Ops must send a new offer and collect the next payment.

Case C: Refused Renewal

If the returning student does not want to continue, they can be moved to Cold Lead and followed up later with the refused-renewal workflow.

4. Returning Student Journey

If Waiting for Round: the student already has credit, so Ops only needs scheduling and class placement.

If Renewal Pending: Ops sends the new package, records payment, then the student moves back into the normal path: Waiting for Round -> Ready to Start -> In Classes.

5. Simple Summary

  • Lead enters
  • Placement test
  • Offer
  • Payment
  • Student joins class
  • Student finishes level
  • Either Renewal Pending
  • Or Paid in Full / Waiting for Round
  • Then next class cycle starts
Prepared from the current system workflow in Eighty Twenty Ops. This is a business summary, not a technical specification.
